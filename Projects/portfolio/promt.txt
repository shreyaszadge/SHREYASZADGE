For each frame, combine two masks: the person mask uses rembg with model u2net_human_seg and only_mask=True; the props mask uses pixels whose maximum channel distance from the background colour (sample the corners, about 247) exceeds 14, ramped to 1 by 40. Apply binary_opening(3), retain connected blobs larger than 4000 px, then dilate by 4 to preserve the chair, table, and laptop. Set alpha = max(personMask, propsMask), then apply a Gaussian blur of 0.8. Remove the white fringe by un-premultiplying against white: rgb = (rgb − (1−a)·247)/a where a > 0.05. Erode the alpha using alpha = 0.35·alpha + 0.65·MinFilter(3)(alpha). Remap alpha using alpha = clip((alpha−0.14)/0.86)^1.4. For edge pixels (alpha < 0.95), multiply alpha by 1 − 0.8·clip((lum−150)/70). Resize to a 1600×900 virtual space. For each shot, compute the union bounding box of alpha > 6, crop every frame of that shot to it, and save as WebP (q80, alpha_quality 90) to lg/, plus a half-size copy (q78) to sm/.
